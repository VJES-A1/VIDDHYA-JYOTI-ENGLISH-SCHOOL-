Our motto

The light of knowledge for every child.

Viddhya Jyoti means the light of knowledge. That is the promise in our name, and we hold ourselves to it in every classroom, every term.

Our quality

Six things we do well, one for each point of the star

Good schooling is a habit, repeated daily. These are the habits we keep.

Confident English

English-medium classes that build speaking, reading and writing, alongside strong Nepali.

Caring teachers

Teachers who know each child by name and follow their progress closely.

Discipline and values

Respect, honesty and punctuality taught every day, in class and on the field.

Beyond the textbook

Sports, arts, assemblies and events that let children find what they are good at.

A safe, clean campus

A calm place to learn, where families can trust that their children are looked after.

Parents as partners

Open doors and regular conversations between home and school.
